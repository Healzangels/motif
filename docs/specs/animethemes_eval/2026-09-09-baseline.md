# AnimeThemes coverage baseline — 2026-09-09

Input: production `motif.db` snapshot dated 2026-07-21 (anime section:
1,280 shows, 94 collections; guid coverage tvdb 1,267 / tmdb 1,269 /
imdb 1,266). Bridge: Fribb/anime-lists `anime-list-mini.json` (5.8 MB,
39,304 entries; 7,471 carry `tvdb_id`, 8,455 carry `themoviedb_id`).
AnimeThemes API: JSON:API at api.animethemes.moe, 90 req/min.
Method: guid_tvdb (+ season) → bridge → AniDB ids; fallback guid_tmdb
(tv); two batched API steps (resource → anime id; anime?filter[anime][id]
→ themes with audio); name search (`q=`) for rows with no bridge hit.

| bucket | rows | bridged | AT any audio | AT has OP | clean | glance |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| no theme | 376 | 339 (90%) | 310 (82%) | 300 (79%) | 291 | 4 |
| Plex-served, no TDB link | 328 | 322 (98%) | 306 (93%) | 303 (92%) | 289 | 14 |
| TDB-linked | 419 | 405 (96%) | 399 (95%) | 395 (94%) | 367 | 25 |
| user SET URL / upload | 157 | 144 (91%) | 125 (79%) | 119 (75%) | 112 | 4 |
| all anime shows | 1,280 | 1,210 (94%) | 1,140 (89%) | 1,117 (87%) | 1,059 | 47 |

"clean" = the bridge landed on the season-1 AniDB entry and the years
agree within 1. "glance" = the season-1 entry is absent on AnimeThemes
and the bridge fell through to a later season (44), or the year differs
by more than 1 (3). Name search rescued 14 of 154 unmapped rows.
Multi-season rows (several AniDB entries per Plex show): 298.
OP1 audio for the 300 no-theme rows: ~830 MB. Sample file: 3.7 MB Vorbis.

Misses (no-theme, 66): mostly non-Japanese animation filed in the anime
section (Arcane, Castlevania, Blood of Zeus, Helluva Boss, donghua) and
unreleased 2026 titles — outside AnimeThemes' catalogue by nature.

Year-mismatch examples (bridge fell through): Bleach → BLEACH: Sennen
Kessen-hen (2022); Cardcaptor Sakura → Clear Card-hen (2018); Pokemon →
Pokemon Advanced Generation (2002). These are the "glance" rows.

Regenerate with `tools/animethemes_eval.py --db <motif.db>` (tag 1).
